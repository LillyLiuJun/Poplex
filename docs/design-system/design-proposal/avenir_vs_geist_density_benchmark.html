场景 2 · 连续段落英文解析 (English Reading Flow)
I’m completely snowed under with meetings today.
我今天被各种会议彻底淹没了，精疲力竭。

The idiom "snowed under with" is a vivid conversational metaphor in modern professional English. It conveys having far more obligations or messages than one can realistically manage, much like a house buried under a sudden mountain blizzard. Notice that we say "feeling exhausted" rather than "feeling sleep", as emotional states require true qualitative adjectives in standard predicate structures.
