Poplex

Reference form borrowed: lowercase wordmark, one letter swapped for a symbol, one small floating accent.
Style rebuilt to the product: warm paper ground, high-contrast display serif, brand orange #FF7622 used once and only once per lockup.

A · Aperture — the "o" is a lens

Reads as: camera aperture / eye / a moment being captured — the exact input gesture of Tab 1. The symbol survives at 16px, which the asterisk form does not. Strongest icon-only candidate.

B · Spark — closest to the reference asterisk

Reads as: the reference's move, translated — arms are tapered instead of flat-cut so they belong to a serif, not a geometric sans. The floating dot sits over the l as a stray spark. Most direct homage; least ownable at small sizes.

C · Utterance — the "o" is said out loud

Reads as: a speech bubble hiding inside the letter. Names the actual promise — speaking — without a mouth, a mic, or a globe. Slightly softer / more app-like than A; the tail is the first thing lost when scaled down.

D · Quoted — two marks make the "o"

Reads as: two quotation marks rotating into a circle — language that comes from someone, quoted from your own life. The most typographic of the four; sits comfortably next to a serif because it is punctuation.

E · Variant A on Fraunces + tight sans alternate

Same mark, two type voices. Fraunces keeps the editorial weight of the onboarding screens. Inter tight is the in-app / navbar lockup — the display serif gets muddy below ~20px, so a sans companion is not optional.
